## Key Observations

1. **Bytes ≠ Tokens.** The previous documents consistently conflated byte sizes with token counts. For prose PLAN.md files, tokens are roughly ¼ to ⅓ of the byte count. For structured HLF syntax, tokens are roughly ¼ of the byte count.

2. **Test 1 NL** coordination artifacts average **436 tokens** (not ~1,786). The ~1,786 figure was the average byte size.

3. **Test 1 HLF** `swarm.hlf` is **805 tokens** (not ~3,266). The ~3,266 figure was the byte size.

4. **Test 2 NL** `PLAN.md` is **1,366 tokens** (not ~900 as estimated in `test-2-nl/RESULTS.md`). The NL estimate was ~24% low.

5. **Test 2 HLF** `swarm.hlf` is **2,082 tokens** (not ~2,159 as estimated in `test-2-hlf/RESULTS.md`). The HLF estimate was ~4% high.

6. **Test 3 NL** `PLAN.md` is **1,471 tokens**.

7. **Test 3 HLF** `swarm.hlf` is **1,928 tokens**.

8. **Code + Tests** for the 3-agent cost test total **4,326 tokens** (not ~3,000 as roughly estimated). The exact tokenizer count is **44% higher** than the rough estimate.
